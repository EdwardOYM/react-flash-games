## Design constants

- Views: `start | tutorial | lobby | lobbyJoin | opening | summary | highscore` — `summary` **replaces**
  `results` (it carries the victory/defeat/draw banner, `finalScore`, both totals and the rematch controls).
- The ceremony stays **in-order and mirrored** for now: one `pair-open` / `card-reveal {pairIndex, cardIndex}`
  advances the matching slot in **both** packs for **both** seats (protocol v2, unchanged). **Either seat may
  click either pack**, which is exactly what `tutorialOpen` tells players today ("Either player's reveal counts
  for both seats") — v3 makes clicking ownership-based.
- Rarity order `common < uncommon < rare < ultraRare < illustrationRare`, rarest first in the summary, stable
  within a rarity (pack order), so both seats derive the same order locally with no wire traffic.
- Ceremony `--pkm-card-w` values are unchanged; the summary gets its own smaller token.
- Motion: `.pkm-card-flipper`'s `.55s` transform transition stays the **single** flip implementation; new motion
  lives in `PokemonPackBattleGame.css` scoped to `.ppb-*`, and `prefers-reduced-motion` snaps everything.

## Deferred: protocol v3 — free-order, own-pack opening (NOT in 04.1)

User decision (captured here; built later — stub plan at
`.clinerules/plans/04.2-pack-battle-free-open-plan/04.2-pack-battle-free-open-plan.md`): **each player opens
their own packs in any order they want, and it still shows on both screens** — if player 1 opens their pack 1,
player 1's pack 1 also opens on player 2's screen; if player 2 opens pack 4, that pack 4 opens on player 1's
screen too.

What the follow-up needs (recorded so it is never re-derived):

- Ownership already exists in the data: `seatForPack(packIndex, packCount)` → host = even, guest = odd, and the
  trailing pack of an odd count belongs to **both**.
- Replace the linear `{pairIndex, cardIndex, opened}` cursor with per-pack state — `{ opened: boolean,
  revealedSlots: number[] }` keyed by pack index.
- Protocol **v3**: per-pack `pack-open {packIndex}` + `card-reveal {packIndex, cardIndex}`, plus an idempotent
  `ceremony-sync` (union-merge snapshot) so a re-dialled seat rebuilds the whole set; bump
  `PACK_BATTLE_PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 3 (the existing version guard already rejects mismatched builds).
- Totals sum the revealed slots per pack (identity scoring unchanged); completion = every pack opened and
  every slot revealed.
- Stage redesign: the ceremony can no longer be gated by round order, so each seat's packs need their own
  panels/pagination (36 packs = 18 per seat) and the "Round {current} of {total}" progress copy is replaced.
- 04.1 keeps its clickable packs/cards and its `summary` page; v3 changes *who* may click and *in what order*,
  not the page flow.
