## Goal

Let each player open and reveal **their own packs**, in **any order**, with every action still mirrored to the
other screen — replacing the current linear "one round = one pack per seat, revealed in lockstep" cursor.

## Required inputs (user)

- Each player opens their **own** packs only, **in any order they want**.
- It **still shows on both screens**: if player 1 opens their pack 1, player 1's pack 1 opens on player 2's
  screen too; if player 2 opens pack 4, that pack 4 opens on player 1's screen too.
- Both seats keep playing simultaneously — no shared round cursor, no waiting for the other seat's turn.

## Why this is a separate plan

- **Protocol change**: v2's `pair-open {pairIndex}` / `card-reveal {pairIndex, cardIndex}` describe a *round*;
  free-order needs per-pack messages plus a union/snapshot replay for re-dialled seats.
- **State model change**: `{pairIndex, cardIndex, opened}` → per-pack `{opened, revealedSlots}`.
- **Stage redesign**: packs can no longer be presented one round at a time, so each seat needs its own pack
  panels with pagination/scrolling (36 packs = 18 per seat), plus new progress copy.
- **Copy/rules change**: `packProgress` ("Round {current} of {total}"), `packsLeft` and `tutorialOpen` all
  change meaning.

## Sketch (to be broken into checkpoints when scheduled)

1. `net/protocol.ts` → v3 messages (`pack-open`, `card-reveal`, `ceremony-sync`) + guards; version 3.
2. `PokemonPackBattleGame.tsx` → per-pack state, ownership gates via `seatForPack` (the odd tail pack is
   openable by either seat; first click wins and union-merges), totals derived from the per-pack sets.
3. Ceremony layout → per-seat pack panels, pagination/scrolling, new progress copy + new en/ms/zh keys.
4. Re-dial → `ceremony-sync` snapshot replay instead of the pair/card cursor replay.
5. Diagram (view machine + data flow) updated; confirm no `AppConfig` change is needed.
6. Validation: build + lint + a render/logic probe over a real seeded pack, viewport audit, human QA.
