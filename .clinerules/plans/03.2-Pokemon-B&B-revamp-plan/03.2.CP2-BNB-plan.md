# 03.2.CP2 — B&B Energy Catalog and Exact 40-Card Deck

> Parent: `03.2-Pokemon-B&B-revamp-plan.md`. Depends on CP1.

## Scope

Implement the locked construction format:

- expose a stable catalog of every available basic Energy type from the existing synthetic Energy pool;
- allow unlimited Energy copies, with technical IDs and deterministic ordering;
- keep opened non-Energy selection separate in builder state, then serialize one combined deck;
- replace `minDeckSize` with `DECK_SIZE = 40` and exact-total legality;
- reject 39/41 totals, missing Basic, unknown IDs, over-pool non-Energy copies, and invalid Energy entries;
- do not make Prize setting affect deck legality.

Add a translated Energy selector/count section to `PokemonBnbGame.tsx`, show opened/Energy/total/40 counts,
and disable submission until exact legality passes.

## Files

`src/games/pokemon-bnb/deck.ts`, `src/games/pokemon-bnb/pack.ts`,
`src/games/pokemon-bnb/PokemonBnbGame.tsx`, and locale files.

## Validation

Pure tests for 39/40/41, all Energy types, duplicate Energy, non-Energy copy limits, missing Basic, and
both Prize settings. `setupBattle` and `deck-ready` must defensively reject non-40 decks. Build + lint.
