In the Pokémon TCG, Abilities provide passive or active utility effects, while Attacks are the primary actions used to damage opponents and win the game.
------------------------------
## 🧬 Rules for Pokémon Abilities
Abilities are special traits printed on a card (indicated by a red or blue banner next to the ability name). They are not attacks and do not require Energy to use.

* 
* When to use: Most Abilities state they can be used "Once during your turn, before your attack."
* From the Bench: Unless a card explicitly states otherwise, Abilities can be used whether the Pokémon is in the Active Spot or on your Bench.
* Special Conditions Block: If a Pokémon is Asleep, Confused, or Paralyzed, its Abilities usually still function unless the card text specifically states otherwise. However, certain card effects (like [Neutralization Zone](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/)) can shut down Abilities entirely.
* Stacking: If you have multiple copies of the same Pokémon in play, you can use each of their Abilities once per turn, unless the card text explicitly says: "You can only use 1 [Ability Name] ability per turn."
*

## ⚔️ Rules for Pokémon Attacks
Declaring an attack is always the very last action of your turn. Once you announce an attack, your turn ends immediately after resolving it.
To successfully execute an attack, you must follow these specific criteria and steps:
## 1. Check Attack Requirements

* 
* Energy Cost: You must have the correct amount and type of Energy attached to your Active Pokémon.
* A white star symbol (Colorless) can be fulfilled by any Energy type.
   * Specific type symbols (e.g., Fire, Water) must match exactly.
* Position: Only your Active Pokémon can attack. Benched Pokémon cannot attack unless an explicit card effect permits it.
* Turn Restrictions: You cannot attack on Turn 1 if you are the player going first.
* 

## 2. Resolve the Attack Steps
When executing the attack, follow this precise order to calculate damage:

| Step | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read Text | Read the attack text and apply any immediate requirements (e.g., "Flip a coin," "Discard an Energy"). |
| 2 | Base Damage | Look at the printed number to the right of the attack name. This is your starting damage. |
| 3 | Damage Modifiers | Apply effects on the attacking Pokémon first (e.g., choice belts, positive/negative damage modifications from previous turns). |
| 4 | Weakness | Check the defending Pokémon's Weakness. If it matches your attacker's type, multiply the damage by 2. |
| 5 | Resistance | Check the defending Pokémon's Resistance. If it matches, subtract the listed amount (usually -30) from the total. |
| 6 | Place Damage | Convert the final damage into damage counters (1 counter = 10 damage) and place them on the defending Pokémon. |

------------------------------
## ⚠️ Special Conditions Affecting Attacks
If your Active Pokémon is suffering from a Special Condition, it directly alters how or if you can attack:

* 
* Asleep / Paralyzed: The Pokémon cannot attack or retreat at all.
* Confused: If you attempt to attack, you must flip a coin.
* Heads: The attack works normally.
   * Tails: The attack fails, and you must place 3 damage counters (30 damage) on your own attacking Pokémon.
