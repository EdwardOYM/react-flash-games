During your turn in the Pokémon TCG, your actions are divided into three distinct phases. You must follow the phases in order, but the actions within the main turn phase can be done in any order you choose.

+-----------------------+

|  1. DRAW PHASE        | -> Must draw 1 card from your deck to start.
+-----------------------+
            │
            ▼
+-----------------------+

|  2. MAIN TURN PHASE   | -> Do any of these actions, in any order,
|                       |    as many times as allowed.
+-----------------------+
            │
            ▼
+-----------------------+

|  3. ATTACK PHASE      | -> Declare an attack, place damage, and
|                       |    END YOUR TURN.
+-----------------------+

------------------------------

* Draw a Card: You must draw the top card of your deck. If your deck is empty and you cannot draw, you instantly lose the game (known as "Decking Out").

------------------------------
You can perform the following actions in any order you like during this phase:
## 🟢 Unlimited Actions (Do as many times as you want)

* Play Basic Pokémon: Put Basic Pokémon from your hand face-up onto an empty slot on your Bench.
* Evolve Pokémon: Place an Evolution card on top of a matching Pokémon in play. (Restriction: A Pokémon cannot evolve on its very first turn in play, or on Turn 1 of the game).
* Play Item Cards: Play as many Item cards from your hand as you like, following the instructions on the card.
* Play Tool Cards: Attach Pokémon Tools to any of your Pokémon in play that don't already have one.
* Use Abilities: Activate the abilities of your Pokémon in play if they say "During your turn...".

## 🔴 Restricted Actions (Only ONCE per turn)

* Attach Energy: Attach exactly 1 Energy card from your hand to 1 of your Pokémon in play.
* Play a Supporter Card: Play only 1 Supporter card per turn. (Restriction: The player going first cannot play a Supporter on Turn 1).
* Play a Stadium Card: Play only 1 Stadium card per turn. You cannot play a Stadium if a copy with the exact same name is already in play.
* Retreat Active Pokémon: Pay the retreat cost (in Energy) listed on your Active Pokémon to switch it with one of your Benched Pokémon.

------------------------------
This is always the final action of your turn:

* Attack: Choose an attack from your Active Pokémon. Check if you have the required Energy attached, calculate damage/effects, apply Weakness/Resistance, and place damage counters. Your turn immediately ends after the attack is completed.
* Pass: If you cannot or do not want to attack, you can simply choose to pass, which also ends your turn.

(Note: The player who goes first cannot attack on their very first turn of the game).
