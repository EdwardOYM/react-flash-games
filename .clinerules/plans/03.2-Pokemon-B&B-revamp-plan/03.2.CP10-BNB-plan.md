## Defects the audit found and fixed

1. **Every disabled control announced the wrong reason.** All twelve action-bar buttons pointed
   `aria-describedby` at one shared node holding only the "choose a hand card" reason, so a screen
   reader heard a reason belonging to a different action for ten of twelve controls (Retreat,
   Attack, Pass and the per-attack buttons all announced "Choose a hand card first"). Each control
   now carries its own visually-hidden reason node and references that.
2. **Attack buttons displayed another control's reason.** They reused the shared hint instead of the
   `beginAttack` reason.
3. **Pass claimed "It is not your turn" when it was the player's own turn**, simply because the phase
   was wrong. `controls.ts` now distinguishes an ownership failure (`not-your-turn`) from a phase
   failure (`not-main-phase`).
4. **`role="img"` on an `<article>`.** The role overrode the sectioning role and the accessibility tree
   fell back to the decorative card-back mark, so the card's `aria-label` name was lost. `PokemonCard`
   now renders a `div` with `role="img"`, the canonical pattern. This affected **every card in the
   whole app**, not just B&B.
5. **The four dev-harness `<select>`s had no accessible name at all** (the `select-name` audit
   failure). They now have translated labels.

The three Lighthouse failures that remain — `meta-description`, `robots-txt`, `llms-txt` — are
site-wide SEO/agentic audits unrelated to B&B and out of this checkpoint's scope.
