# 03.2.CP7 — B&B Action Controls and Promotions

> Parent: `03.2-Pokemon-B&B-revamp-plan.md`. Depends on CP6.

## Scope

Build controls for the new actions: hand/target selection, Basic-to-Bench, Energy, evolution, Item,
Supporter, Stadium, Tool, Ability, Retreat, Attack, Pass, and promotion. Illegal actions stay disabled or
hidden with a translated reason. Selection clears only after authoritative success. Support one pending
side, both pending after simultaneous KO, next-player-first promotion order, and no unrelated actions
between KO resolution and promotion. Keep `?local=1` on the shared `processAction` driver.

## Files

`PokemonBnbGame.tsx`, co-located styles, action/keyboard wiring, locales.

## Validation

Every action has success/rejection coverage; host/guest intent behavior; local harness parity; selection
lifecycle; single and simultaneous promotion ordering; build + lint.
