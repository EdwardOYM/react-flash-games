- Bump B&B `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 2; reject version-1 peers.
- Change Prize settings from a 2–6 range to exactly 4 or 6, default 4.

- Replace the lobby stepper with a 4/6 segmented choice and preserve host broadcast/guest clamp.
- Add all later-player-facing copy to en/ms/zh and the translation union before rendering it: deck 40,
  Energy construction, setup, phases, actions, statuses, board zones, and reveal copy.
