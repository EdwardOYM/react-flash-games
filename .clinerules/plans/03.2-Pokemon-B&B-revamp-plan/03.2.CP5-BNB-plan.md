# 03.2.CP5 — B&B Abilities and Attack Resolution

> Parent: `03.2-Pokemon-B&B-revamp-plan.md`. Depends on CP4.

## Scope

Add a player-triggered Ability action and deterministic trigger/effect registry. Abilities are not
attacks, consume no Energy, may be used from Active/Bench when card text permits, normally remain usable
under Special Conditions, and obey per-copy/once-per-name restrictions. Preserve text as data and classify
unsupported clauses explicitly; never guess.

Validate attacks as Active-only, exact typed/Colorless Energy costs, first-player Turn-1 restriction,
Asleep/Paralyzed rejection, and seeded Confusion heads/tails with 30 self-damage. Resolve in this order:
requirements, base damage, attacker modifiers, Weakness, Resistance, damage, effects, KO/prizes, end turn.

## Files

Engine effects/actions/types, card/effect audit, ability/action UI, locales.

## Validation

Typed/Colorless costs, first-turn and status rejection, both Confusion outcomes, Weakness/Resistance
ordering, modifiers, KO/simultaneous KO, every supported effect ID, unsupported coverage report, build + lint.

## Progress

- [x] CP5 completed 2026-09-25.

## What shipped

- `game-core/effects.ts` — Ability registry: `AbilityEffect` (data), `ABILITY_EFFECTS` pattern
  table, `classifyAbility`, `isPlayerTriggeredAbility`, `abilityCoverageReport`, and
  `applyAbilityEffect` (seeded search / attach-from-hand / search-and-attach / heal).
  `plainCardText` now folds diacritics so "Pokémon" matches ASCII patterns.
- `game-core/actions.ts` — `activateAbility` resolves the effect on a clone first, so an unmet
  requirement rejects the action without mutating state; `processAction` forwards `targetIndex`.
- `game-core/types.ts` — `SideState.abilityUsedNames` (per-side, per-Ability-name once-per-turn)
  and optional `targetIndex` on the `useAbility` action.
- `PokemonBnbGame.tsx` — `abilityTarget()` supplies the chosen Pokemon for "1 of your Pokemon"
  heals; Active/Bench Ability buttons pass it through.
- en/ms/zh — 4 log keys + 3 error keys, plus the key union in `assets/languages/index.ts`.

## Coverage (verified against 30C data)

16 distinct printed Ability texts across 23 cards: 6 player-triggered and fully resolved, 10 passive
(HP bonus, damage prevention, Bench cost reduction, KO reactions) classified `unsupported` and
rejected by the `ability-ineligible` gate rather than guessed.

## Validation

- `scripts/cp5-assertions.ts` (throwaway, deleted after the run) — 32 assertions passed, covering
  coverage totals, all four supported effects, once-per-copy and once-per-name limits, phase/turn
  restrictions, seed determinism, typed/colorless costs, first-turn and Asleep/Paralyzed
  rejection, both Confusion outcomes, and non-negative damage.
- en/ms/zh parity: 608 keys, 0 missing.
- `npm run build` passed; `npm run lint` 0 warnings / 0 errors.

## Known limitations

- Passive Abilities are not simulated; they are reported, not approximated.
- Ability effects resolve the *first* matching deck card (deterministic), since CP7 owns the
  deck-search selection UI.
