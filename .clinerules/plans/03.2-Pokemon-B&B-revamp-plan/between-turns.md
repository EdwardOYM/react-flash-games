The Between-Turns Phase (formerly known as the Pokémon Checkup) happens after a player ends their turn but before the next player draws a card.
Even though it occurs between turns, this phase does not belong to either player, and it follows a strict, step-by-step order to resolve ongoing card conditions.

[1. Resolve Special Conditions] ──> [2. Resolve Abilities/Effects] ──> [3. Check for Knockouts]
                                                                                │
[Next Player's Turn Begins] <───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

------------------------------
Check your Active Pokémon and your opponent's Active Pokémon for any Special Conditions. You must resolve them in this exact order:

   1. Poisoned: Place 1 damage counter (10 damage) on the Poisoned Pokémon. (Some card effects or abilities can increase this damage).
   2. Burned: Place 2 damage counters (20 damage) on the Burned Pokémon. Then, the owner of that Pokémon flips a coin. If heads, the Pokémon recovers from being Burned; if tails, it stays Burned.
   3. Asleep: The owner of the Asleep Pokémon flips a coin. If heads, the Pokémon wakes up; if tails, it stays Asleep.
   4. Paralyzed: If a Pokémon was Paralyzed during the turn just ended, it now recovers and returns to normal.

------------------------------
Next, apply the effects of any active Pokémon Abilities or Trainer cards (like Stadiums) that explicitly state they function "during Pokémon Checkup" or "between turns."

* 
* Note: If both players have effects that trigger at this exact moment, the player whose turn just ended chooses the order in which their own cards resolve first, followed by the opponent resolving theirs.
* 

------------------------------
Finally, look at the board state to see if any Pokémon has damage counters equal to or exceeding its maximum HP.

* 
* If a Pokémon is Knocked Out: Move it and all attached cards to its owner's Discard Pile.
* Prize Cards: Players take their Prize Cards for any Knockouts caused during this phase.
* Simultaneous KOs: If both players' Active Pokémon are Knocked Out at the exact same time (e.g., due to Poison), both players choose a new Active Pokémon from their Bench, starting with the player whose turn is about to begin.
* 

Once all three steps are complete, the next player's turn officially begins, and they draw their card.
------------------------------
