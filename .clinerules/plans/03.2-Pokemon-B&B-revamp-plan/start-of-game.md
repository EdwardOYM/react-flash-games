Explanation:
Here is the step-by-step start of game setup flow for a standard Pokémon TCG match, arranged sequentially from the initial handshake to the first draw.

[1. Shuffle & Cut] ──> [2. Flip Coin] ──> [3. Draw 7 Cards] ──> [4. Place Active/Bench]
                                                                        │
[8. Start Game!] <── [7. Reveal Cards] <── [6. Set 6 Prizes] <─── [5. Handle Mulligans]

------------------------------
## Phase 1: Preparation

* 1. Shuffle and Cut: Thoroughly shuffle your 60-card deck. Offer your opponent the chance to cut or shuffle your deck, and do the same for theirs.
* 2. Coin Flip: Flip a coin (or roll an official die). The winner of the flip chooses who goes first or second.
* Note: This choice must be made before looking at your opening hand.

## Phase 2: Drawing & Pokémon Placement

* 3. Draw Hand: Both players draw the top 7 cards of their deck to form their starting hand.
* 4. Check for Basic Pokémon: Look at your hand for a Basic Pokémon.
* If you have one: Place one Basic Pokémon face-down into your Active Spot. You may also place any additional Basic Pokémon from your hand face-down onto your Bench (up to 5).
   * If you don't have one: You have a Mulligan (proceed to Phase 3).

## Phase 3: Resolving Mulligans

* 5. Handle Mulligans: If you do not have any Basic Pokémon in your initial 7 cards:
* Reveal your hand to your opponent to prove you have no Basic Pokémon.
   * Shuffle your hand back into your deck and draw a new hand of 7 cards.
   * Repeat this process until you find at least one Basic Pokémon and place it face-down as your Active Pokémon.
   * The Penalty: For every time you had to shuffle back, your opponent may choose to draw 1 extra card from their deck. (They draw these extra cards after setting up their own Prize Cards).

## Phase 4: Final Setup

* 6. Set Prize Cards: Place the top 6 cards of your deck face-down to the side to act as your Prize Cards. Do not look at them.
* 7. Reveal Pokémon: Both players flip their Active and Benched Pokémon face-up at the same time.
* 8. Start the Game: The player chosen to go first begins their turn by drawing a card.

------------------------------
## ⚠️ Turn 1 Constraints to Remember
If you go first, you are restricted on your very first turn:

* You cannot attack.
* You cannot play Supporter cards from your hand.
* Neither player can evolve any Pokémon on their very first turn in play.
