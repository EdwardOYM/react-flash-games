# 04.7 — Implement the pure-damage attack effects (pokemon-bnb, 30c)

> **Checkpoint protocol**: run ONE checkpoint per session turn. After each, run the narrowest
> relevant validation (`npm run build`, plus `npm run lint` for code), report files + behaviour +
> result, then STOP and wait for "next".
>
> **Scope, corrected by measurement (2026-09-26).** The user asked to implement the ability and attack
> effects of all 30c Pokémon and Trainer cards, possibly split across subagents. Two limits were
> established before any code was written, and they reshape the request:
>
> - **No subagent tool exists here** (only edit / search / terminal / browser tools), so the work is
>   structured into independent checkpoints instead of being farmed out.
> - **Measured against the real data, not assumed.** 224 attack instances, 154 with printed text, **99
>   distinct texts, 85 with an unsupported clause, affecting 119 attack instances.** Categorised:
>   **pure maths 19 texts / 21 instances**, **needs new engine state 5 / 10**, **needs player choice or
>   a UI picker 61 / 88.**
>
> **Correction to the estimate given to the user before measuring:** I said the first slice was "~20-25
> of the 85 texts". The measured pure-maths total is **19 texts / 21 instances**, and re-reading them
> shows several of the `discardEnergy` texts are *compound* (they also heal a Benched Pokémon or spread
> damage, i.e. player choice), so the genuinely pure slice is smaller still. This plan is scoped to what
> the data supports and says so rather than padding the number.
>
> **Two parts of the original ask are already resolved, so they are NOT in this plan:**
> - **Trainer cards: nothing to do.** 30c contains **3** trainer cards and **0** of them has printed
>   effect text, so there is no trainer effect to implement for this set.
> - **Player-triggered Abilities: already done.** The ability registry reports **supported 6, passive 10,
>   player-triggered-and-missing 0**. All six "Once during your turn" abilities already resolve. The
>   remaining ten are *passive/continuous* clauses (prevent-all-damage while benched, Retreat Cost
>   reduction, heal-if-still-Asleep at Checkup, borrow a Benched Pokémon's attacks, and so on) that
>   need **new engine state and new hooks**, not new effect entries. They are out of this plan and are
>   the obvious next plan if this one lands.

## What CP1 covers (each is damage maths over state the engine already holds)

| New `ParsedEffect` kind | Printed text it handles | Texts |
|---|---|---|
| `bonusDamagePerEnergyType` | "30 more damage for each **Water** Energy attached to this Pokémon" | 1 |
| `bonusDamagePerDamageCounter` | "10 more damage for each damage counter on this Pokémon" | 1 |
| `bonusDamageIfHasEnergyType` | "If this Pokémon has any **Lightning** Energy attached, this attack does 80 more damage" | 2 |
| `bonusDamagePerOwnCount` | "30 damage for each of your Pokémon in play" / "…for each of your Pikachu and Pikachu ex in play" | 2 |
| `bonusDamagePerTypeCount` | "50 damage for each type of Basic Energy attached to all of your Pokémon" | 1 |
| `bonusDamagePerDiscardEnergy` | "20 more damage for each Energy card in your discard pile" | 1 |
| `bonusDamagePerDefenderAttached` | "40 more damage for each Energy attached to your opponent's Active Pokémon" | 1 |
| `bonusDamageIfDefenderIsEx` | "If your opponent's Active Pokémon is a Pokémon ex, this attack does 80 more damage" | 1 |
| `flipUntilTailsDamage` | "Flip a coin until you get tails. This attack does 20 damage for each heads" | 1 |
| `extraPrizeOnKo` | "If your opponent's Pokémon is Knocked Out by damage from this attack, take 1 more Prize card" | 1 |

Two of these lean on work already shipped: `bonusDamagePerDamageCounter` uses **04.5's** damage
counters, and `bonusDamageIfDefenderIsEx` reads the **04.6 `suffix`** field that 04.6 added — this is
exactly the "rule box" data, reused for a condition instead of a Prize take.

## Locked rules for CP1

- **Every new kind is `beforeDamage`.** All ten adjust the attack's damage before Weakness/Resistance,
  so `effectTiming` gains no new branch and damage stays a single number flowing through the existing
  `computeAttackDamage`.
- **A near-miss stays unsupported.** Each family's regex is anchored end-to-end. If a clause cannot be
  read exactly, the text keeps reporting `unsupported` and nothing is applied. The harness asserts this
  for every family with a deliberately-mutated near-miss, because a silently-wrong effect is the worst
  possible outcome.
- **`flipUntilTailsDamage` is bounded.** The coin loop caps at a fixed number of flips so a pathological
  seed cannot hang the match; the cap is a named constant with the reason in a comment.
- **`extraPrizeOnKo` is the one entry that touches `performKo`.** It reuses 04.6's
  `prizesForKnockOut` take loop and its rule-box log, and the bonus is a **plain extra take**, not a new
  rule box. It fires only when the knockout came from the attack being resolved, so the KO path needs the
  attacker context passed down — the smallest structural change in this plan, and it is called out here
  rather than discovered mid-implementation.
- **Do NOT** add: deck searches (need a player-choice picker, which the pure synchronous engine cannot
  express), spread/direct damage to a chosen Pokémon (same), switch/devolve/shuffle-self (board
  manipulation), anything needing "during your opponent's next turn" state, or the ten passive Abilities.

## Deferred, with the reason recorded

- **61 texts / 88 instances needing player choice or a picker UI** (deck search, spread damage, heal a
  chosen Pokémon, switch, devolve, shuffle-self, place counters, self-damage recoil). These are
  *interaction* features, not effect-parsing features, and the engine is a pure synchronous function with
  no interaction seam. They need their own plan and a UI design.
- **5 texts / 10 instances needing new state** ("Knocked Out last turn", "damaged by an attack last
  turn", "can't attack next turn", "attacks do N less damage"). Each is a small per-turn or per-mon flag;
  a contained plan, but genuinely new state.
- **10 passive Abilities** — new hooks in `resolveAttack` / `performKo` / `retreatToBench` /
  `applyCheckup`.
- **Self-Knock-Out recoil** is called out by the engine header as deliberately not implemented, so
  `selfDamage` is treated as structural, not arithmetic.
